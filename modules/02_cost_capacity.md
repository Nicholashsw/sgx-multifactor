# Module 2. Cost and capacity waterfall

Finding: the broad-universe composite is statistically real and economically untradeable. Gross 1.661%/mo (t=5.13) falls to 0.366%/mo (t=1.08) after Corwin-Schultz spreads on the names traded (0.741%/mo, the dominant leak; traded-days median half-spread 36.4 bp), the Markit availability screen, and fees. Across spread estimators and charging rules the fully costed alpha stays between 0.06 and 0.54%/mo, never significant, and the long-only top decile keeps 0.09%/mo (t=0.36) after spreads. The short side is availability-constrained, not fee-constrained: the bottom two cap quintiles hold effectively zero lendable inventory, and requiring real inventory mechanically forces the short book into large caps, where it survives to S$500m AUM (implementable L/S +0.764%/mo t=2.23 at S$50m). On the price-signal book, long-only (+0.713%/mo, t=4.19, Sharpe 0.85) dominates the borrow-feasible long-short. STI-core capacity is ~S$50M, feasibility-bound rather than cost-bound.
