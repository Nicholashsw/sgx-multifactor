# Module 6. Price-signal family (52-week high, reversal)

The 52-week-high and momentum signals subsume each other conditionally on share turnover (a mechanism corrected twice: breadth, then liquidity, then turnover, with a 12-subset placebo confirming the screen drives it). Low-turnover names: momentum subsumes 52WH (residual t=5.67, survives every gross correction). High-turnover liquid names: the 52WH spread (+1.45%/mo gross, t=3.74) clears Bonferroni and the bootstrap reality check; its residual over momentum clears only BHY/HLZ. Net of spreads nothing in the family survives any correction (0 of 22). A statistical finding about signal structure, not tradable alpha.
